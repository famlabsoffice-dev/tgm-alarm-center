## About the app

TGM ALARM CENTER helps you plan local alarms, warnings, and recurring gaming-event cycles. Alarm, profile, and settings data are designed to remain on your device. The app is not an official part of any third-party game.

## Create an alarm

Open **Alarms**, select **New alarm**, enter a name, choose the date, time, recurrence, warnings, and sound, then save the alarm. The alarm should appear in your overview and be scheduled according to your settings.

## Enable notifications

The app requires notification permission to show local reminders. If permission was denied, open your device settings, select TGM ALARM CENTER, and enable notifications.

On Android, exact alarms and battery-optimization settings may also affect delivery. Device manufacturers use different names for these settings.

## If an alarm does not fire

Check that the alarm is enabled, the device date, time, and time zone are correct, and notification permission is active. On Android, review exact-alarm and battery-optimization settings. Open the app, change and save the alarm again, and run a short test alarm.

On iOS, check notification permission, Focus mode, Scheduled Summary, Silent mode, and volume. The app cannot display a notification if the operating system blocks it or the device is powered off.

## GW five-day cycle

The GW five-day cycle schedules an alarm every five days and displays its protection window and next planned occurrences. Review the next occurrence after changing the cycle. The calculation uses the device’s local time and calendar settings.

## Backup and restore

Use **Settings > Backup & Restore** to export alarm data and settings. Keep the exported file in a secure location. Import only files created by TGM ALARM CENTER and do not share passwords, payment data, or store verification tokens.

Backups are not automatically synchronized across devices. Cloud or file-sharing providers apply their own terms and privacy policies.

## Delete local data

Use **Delete all local data** in Settings to remove alarms, profiles, settings, and local history from the device. This action cannot be undone. Copies of exported backups stored elsewhere must be deleted separately.

## Purchases and plans

When paid plans are enabled, purchases are processed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. If a purchase does not appear after payment, open the plan area and select **Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase.

Apple and Google handle receipts, refunds, billing details, and subscription cancellation. For technical purchase issues, contact us without sending payment-card details or verification tokens.
